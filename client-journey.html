Structured Client Engagement Pathway

A controlled, consultant-led process designed for private and institutional clients.

Initial Private Consultation

Prospective clients submit a confidential request through the approved consultant channel. Basic profile indicators are reviewed to determine preliminary suitability.

  • Low-friction
  • Confidential
  • No obligation
Eligibility & Structural Assessment

The consultant conducts a structured review of the client profile, jurisdictional needs, and banking compatibility factors.

  • Client category
  • Cross-border requirements
  • Structural complexity
  • Preliminary risk considerations

Not all enquiries proceed beyond this stage.

Compliance & Documentation Phase

Where appropriate, clients proceed to formal due diligence including AML, KYC, and jurisdictional compatibility review.

  • Identity verification
  • Source of funds review
  • Structural documentation
  • Jurisdiction matching

This stage follows internationally recognized compliance practices.

Appropriate Account Variant Allocation

Based on the completed assessment, the suitable private banking structure is determined.

  • GPCO
  • SOVRA
  • SDRA
  • FSVA
  • CISA
Account Establishment Coordination

The consultant coordinates the structured onboarding process, ensuring documentation alignment and jurisdictional compatibility throughout.

  • Guided process
  • Controlled timeline
  • Secure handling
Ongoing Client Coordination

Where required, consultants remain available to assist with operational coordination, structural updates, and cross-border considerations.

Client Categories We Commonly Support

Structured solutions designed for private, institutional, and globally active clients.

Ultra High Net Worth Individuals

For globally active private clients requiring structured banking access and wealth coordination.

Family Offices

Supporting multi-generational wealth structures and cross-jurisdiction financial architecture.

Corporate Treasury Groups

For internationally operating companies requiring structured banking alignment.

Investment & Asset Structures

For complex ownership environments requiring coordinated financial positioning.

Selective Engagement Model

Our framework is designed for structured private banking environments and may not be suitable for retail or entry-level banking needs.

Each enquiry is reviewed individually, and progression depends on jurisdictional compatibility, documentation readiness, and institutional acceptance criteria.
