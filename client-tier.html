Private Client Classification Framework

To ensure appropriate structuring and service alignment, client engagements are categorized under a tiered private client framework.

Tier classification reflects cross-border complexity, asset profile, and structural requirements.

Alpha
Private Client Level
Typical Profile
  • Emerging international clients
  • Structured business owners
  • Cross-border entrepreneurs
Indicative Focus
  • Entry private banking structuring
  • GPCO-class alignment
  • Basic cross-border coordination
Handling Level
  • Standard consultant handling
  • Structured onboarding
  • Jurisdiction matching
Sigma
High Net Worth Level
Typical Profile
  • Established HNW individuals
  • Multi-entity business operators
  • Active cross-border participants
Indicative Focus
  • Multi-account structuring
  • SOVRA / SDRA compatibility
  • Enhanced coordination support
Handling Level
  • Priority consultant review
  • Expanded structuring scope
  • Risk mapping support
Omega
Ultra High Net Worth Level
Typical Profile
  • UHNW individuals
  • Family offices
  • Investment syndicates
Indicative Focus
  • FSVA-class structures
  • Advanced custody architecture
  • Multi-jurisdiction coordination
Handling Level
  • Senior consultant handling
  • Complex structuring
  • Dedicated case oversight
Sovereign
Institutional / Strategic Level
Typical Profile
  • Large corporate groups
  • Institutional entities
  • Complex global structures
Indicative Focus
  • Institutional-scale architecture
  • CISA layered structures
  • Strategic financial coordination
Handling Level
  • Executive-level handling
  • Multi-layer structuring
  • Strategic oversight
Tier placement is determined solely following consultant assessment and institutional compatibility review.

Tier Assignment Methodology

Client tier classification is based on a combination of structural complexity, cross-border requirements, asset positioning, and jurisdictional considerations.

The framework is designed to ensure that each client is aligned with the appropriate level of handling and account architecture.

How Tiering Works
  • Cross-border activity level
  • Structural complexity
  • Asset positioning
  • Jurisdictional requirements
  • Documentation readiness
